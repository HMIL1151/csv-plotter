CSV Plotter – quick guide

Plotting

  • Open or drop a CSV, pick the X column, tick columns to plot.
  • Drag on the plot to zoom, double-click to reset, scroll to zoom.
  • Labels tab: title, axis labels/units, series colours. Drag ⠿ to reorder series (and their axes).
  • Style tab: themes, palettes, overlaid / shared / stacked layouts, export size.

Annotations

  • Pick a tool, then click or drag on the plot. Arrows and markers snap to the nearest data point (hold Alt to place freely). Hold Shift to keep the tool active.
  • Drag anything to move it; drag span/rectangle edges to resize; drag labels to move just the text.
  • Double-click a label to edit its text in place. Right-click for more options.
  • Text supports <b>, <i>, <sup>, <sub>.
